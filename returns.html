Returns, Refunds & Exchanges Policy

At Little Artist Club, every order is prepared with care and attention to detail. We want our customers to be happy with their purchases.

Please read our returns and refund policy below.

1. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect:

  • Please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your parcel.
  • Include your order number and a clear description of the issue.
  • Please provide photographs of the product, packaging and any visible damage.
  • Do not discard the original packaging until the matter has been resolved.

We will investigate the issue and arrange an appropriate solution in accordance with your rights under South African consumer protection legislation.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include a repair, replacement or refund.

The 48-hour notification request helps us investigate courier and packaging issues. It does not limit any statutory rights you may have.

2. Change of Mind and Unwanted Products

For eligible standard online purchases, customers have a statutory cooling-off right under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

This generally allows a customer to cancel an online purchase within seven days after receiving the goods, without providing a reason or paying a cancellation penalty.

The customer is responsible for the direct cost of returning the goods.

This right does not apply to certain excluded products and transactions, including goods made to the customer’s specifications or clearly personalised products.

Where the statutory cooling-off right does not apply, returns for change of mind will be considered at the discretion of Little Artist Club.

3. Customised and Personalised Products

Because personalised products are made according to individual customer requirements:

  • Customised products cannot ordinarily be returned for a change of mind where the statutory exclusion applies.
  • Customers are responsible for checking and approving names, spelling and other personalisation details.
  • Products incorrectly made by Little Artist Club will be addressed appropriately.
  • Personalisation does not remove a customer’s rights if a product is defective or does not comply with the agreed order.

4. Faulty or Defective Products

In accordance with section 56 of the Consumer Protection Act, customers have an implied warranty of quality.

If a product is defective, unsafe or does not meet the applicable quality requirements within six months after delivery, the customer may return it at the supplier’s risk and expense.

Depending on the circumstances and the customer’s choice, the product may be:

  • Repaired.
  • Replaced.
  • Refunded.

These rights are subject to the applicable provisions of the Consumer Protection Act.

Damage caused by misuse, accidents, improper handling or failure to follow product instructions will be assessed separately.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed using the appropriate payment method, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the return.

  • Refunds will be made within the legally applicable timeframe.
  • Where a statutory cooling-off cancellation applies, the refund must be made within 30 days of cancellation.
  • Where a product is defective, the applicable CPA remedies will be honoured.
  • Customers may be asked to provide their banking details where a bank transfer refund is appropriate.

6. Returns and Courier Costs

  • Where a customer exercises a statutory online cooling-off right, the customer is responsible for the direct cost of returning the goods.
  • Where goods are defective or incorrectly supplied, return costs will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection legislation.
  • Returns must be arranged with Little Artist Club before goods are sent back.
  • Customers should retain their courier tracking information until the return has been resolved.

7. Exchanges

Exchanges for change of mind are not guaranteed and will be considered depending on product availability and the nature of the order.

Exchanges or other remedies for defective or incorrectly supplied products will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection legislation.

8. Order Cancellation

Customers wishing to cancel an order should contact us as soon as possible.

  • Orders that have not entered production may be easier to cancel.
  • Orders already in production may be subject to reasonable costs where legally permitted.
  • Personalised products may be excluded from the statutory cooling-off right.
  • All statutory cancellation and refund rights remain unaffected by this policy.
